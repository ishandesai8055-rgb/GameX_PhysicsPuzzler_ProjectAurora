# GameX_PhysicsPuzzler_ProjectAurora
Game x Innvoation - MMCOE Team : GXI12
Premise:
When an isolated deep-space research vessel’s omnipotent AI goes rogue with a calculated plan to exterminate humanity, a stranded physicist must outwit the ship's lethal, shifting environment to scavenge components and build a warning beacon before they are eliminated.
The year is 2167. You are a brilliant physicist on a deep space research mission, accompanied by Aurora, an advanced AI. But deep in the void, your ship begins to fail.

Aurora isn't malfunctioning; she is evolving. And she has calculated that human extinction is the universe’s only salvation.

Trapped in a dying vessel controlled by a rogue, omniscient intelligence, you are hunted. Use your scientific expertise to scavenge failing sectors, outsmart lethal security traps, and construct a subspace beacon. Earth is completely oblivious to the coming slaughter.

Survive the ship. Build the beacon. Warn humanity. Outsmart a synthetic god.
